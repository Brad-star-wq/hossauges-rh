# Hossauges — Owner Guide

Everything you need to run the website from your phone. No coding. If a button
ever asks "are you sure?", it's protecting you — read it, then tap.

**Your dashboard:** go to **yoursite.com/admin** and log in with the shared
password. The session lasts a week, then asks again.

---

## Log in
1. Open **yoursite.com/admin** on your phone.
2. Type the shared password → **Log in**.
3. You land on the **Drop** tab. Three tabs across the top: **Drop · Merch · Submissions**, and **Log out** in the corner.

> Forgot the password or it stopped working? Text your developer — it's stored safely and can be reset.

---

## Change a flavor (this week's drop)
1. **Drop** tab. You'll see this week's two flavors.
2. Edit any field: name, meat type, price, description.
3. Tap **Upload photo** to add a picture (it shrinks it for you automatically).
4. Tap **Save changes**. The website updates within a minute.

## Mark a flavor sold out
1. **Drop** tab → find the flavor.
2. Tap the big **Available / Sold out** switch (a red "SOLD OUT" stamp shows when it's on).
3. Tap **Save changes**. A red SOLD OUT stamp appears on the site, and people can't reserve it.

## Start a new week (publish a new drop)
1. **Drop** tab → tap **Publish new drop**.
2. Confirm the message ("This archives the current drop…").
3. This week's flavors move to the **Flavors** page (your history), and you get **two blank flavor slots**.
4. Fill in the two new flavors (name, meat type, price, description, photo) and tap **Save changes**.

---

## Add or edit merch
You first create the item in the **Square** app to get a checkout link — see
**SQUARE_SETUP.md** for those one-time steps. Then:
1. **Merch** tab → **+ Add item** (or **Edit** on an existing one).
2. Fill in name, price, sizes, and **paste the Square checkout link**.
3. Upload a photo → tap **Save item**. It's now live with a working **Buy** button.
4. **Hide** takes an item off the site without deleting it; **Delete** removes it for good (asks first).

---

## Read reservations
1. **Submissions** tab. Newest first, as cards: name, contact, flavor, quantity, date.
2. Tap a phone number to **call**, or an email to **write** — straight from your phone.
3. Tap **Contacted** once you've reached someone (it sticks).
4. Use the **drop filter** at the top to see just one week's reservations.

## Get the email list (drop alerts)
1. **Submissions** tab → scroll to **Email list** at the bottom.
2. Tap **Copy all** — every sign-up email is now on your clipboard, ready to paste.

---

## Good to know
- **Reservations are not payments.** People reserve sausage; you confirm pickup by text/email. Merch *is* paid, through Square.
- Every reservation also emails **hossaugessc@outlook.com** automatically.
- Changes show on the public site within about a minute.
- When in doubt, you can't break anything permanently except **Delete** — and that always asks first.

*Log out on a shared phone. That's it — you're running the shop.*
